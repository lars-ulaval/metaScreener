Limitations:
  - Validation N is bounded by the demonstration corpus universe;
    extending kappa across multiple corpora is future work.
  - Overlap subset is randomly selected with stratification on
    stage_outcome; with N=15 per stage the inter-human kappa is
    sensitive to small absolute disagreement counts.
  - Human raters were the three paper authors. Independent
    multi-disciplinary adjudication is not represented here.
